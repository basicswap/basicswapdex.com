what mobile gets you

The same protocol, minus the complexities.

BasicSwap Mobile is a light client. No BasicSwap to install, no node to run, no chain to store. The protocol underneath is unchanged: every swap stays non-custodial, private, and settled on-chain, exactly as it is on Desktop. What differs is how little it asks of your device.

01

Plug and play

No BasicSwap to install and no node to run. The swap surface comes packaged inside an app or website you already use, ready the moment you open it.

02

Nothing to store or sync

No multi-gigabyte blockchain to download, no daemon to keep synced or updated. The light client reads only the chain state it needs from public nodes.

03

Available anywhere

Swap from a browser or phone, wherever you are. The trade never depends on a machine back home being powered on and synced.

04

Wider reach, deeper liquidity

Packaging swaps inside the apps and wallets people already use brings far more participants to the shared order book. More swappers means more counterparties and better fills for you.
