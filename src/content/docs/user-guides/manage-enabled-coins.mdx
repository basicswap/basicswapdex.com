Every coin you enable has a wallet in BasicSwap, and the **Wallets** page is where you watch them sync, fund them and move coins out. BasicSwap's wallets are ordinary wallets of each coin, created from your one recovery phrase and held on your computer, and your swaps pay from and into these same wallets. [Enable or disable coins](/docs/user-guides/enable-disable-coins) explains how to add and remove them.

## Check sync progress

Most coins run a full node, which downloads and checks the coin's whole blockchain before you can use the wallet. The **Wallets** page lets you follow it, with a card for each coin that shows its version, the latest block, when it last updated, and a **Blockchain** progress bar that reaches 100% when the node has caught up. The first sync can take hours for larger chains, and longer on a slow disk or connection.

Coins in [lightweight modes](/docs/user-guides/lightweight-modes) skip this step entirely, so Bitcoin and Litecoin in Electrum mode show the server they're connected to instead, and Monero or Wownero on a remote node rely on that node's sync.

## Manage wallets

Click **Manage Wallet** on a coin's card to open its wallet, which has a section for each of these:

- **Deposit.** Copy the deposit address, or create a new one with **New Deposit Address**. Some coins also have their own address types, such as Monero subaddresses, Litecoin MWEB addresses, Firo Spark addresses and Particl stealth addresses.
- **Withdraw.** Send coins to another wallet by entering the address and amount, or use **Sweep All** to send everything, and tick **Subtract Fee** to take the network fee out of the amount sent.
- **Convert.** On Particl, this moves funds between its plain, blind and anon balances.
- **History.** The **Transaction History** lists past transactions, and **UTXO Groups** shows the outputs your balance is made of.

<Callout type="info" title="Some wallets need a reseed">

If a wallet's card shows **Expected Seed** as **False**, the wallet isn't yet using the keys from your recovery phrase, and BasicSwap hides its addresses until it does. This happens with PIVX, Dash, Firo and Bitcoin Cash, whose wallets can only take the seed once their blockchains have finished syncing. Open the wallet and click **Reseed wallet** once, and after a few seconds the card shows **Expected Seed** as **True**.

</Callout>

## Wallet password

**Change/Set Password** on the Wallets page encrypts every wallet with a password, or changes it. BasicSwap then asks for the password to unlock the wallets, and you need them unlocked to trade. You'll also need it as `WALLET_ENCRYPTION_PWD` when you [add a coin](/docs/user-guides/enable-disable-coins#enable-a-coin).

## Particl staking

Particl is a proof-of-stake coin, so the PART in your BasicSwap wallet can earn staking rewards while BasicSwap runs. With basicswap-bash, run `bsx-staking` with BasicSwap stopped to turn it on or off. On other installs, set `staking=1` or `staking=0` in `particl/particl.conf` in your data folder, then restart BasicSwap.
