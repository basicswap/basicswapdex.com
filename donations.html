donations · community-funded

No protocol fees.
No investors.

BasicSwap is open-source software written and maintained by an independent community. There is no token, no premine, no equity round, and no protocol cut at swap time. The work is paid for by direct community support, in the cryptocurrencies the protocol itself helps trade.

where it goes

What your support funds.

  1. 01 · maintenance

    Daily upkeep of the protocol, the message mesh, and the lightweight-node integrations.

  2. 02 · research

    Cryptographic work on adaptor-signature variants, SMSG performance, hash-binding edge cases, and new coin integrations.

  3. 03 · docs

    Whitepaper revisions, architecture notes, integration guides, and the public order-book dashboard.

Every contributor and every release is public on GitHub. The work is the receipt: you can see exactly what support funds, commit by commit.

other ways

Code, time, or a quote.

Money is not the only contribution. Reviewing pull requests, filing high-quality bugs, writing docs, or vouching for BasicSwap on a podcast moves the project forward in ways money cannot.
